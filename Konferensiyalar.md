# Sun'iy Intellekt, Mashinali O'rgatish va Ma'lumotlar Ilmi Bo'yicha Xalqaro va Mahalliy Konferensiyalar Katalogi

Ushbu katalog **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, **Machine Learning (ML)**, **Deep Learning (DL)**, **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, **Data Science / Data Analytics**, **Image Processing** va **Computer Vision (CV)** sohalaridagi eng nufuzli TOP-20 xalqaro va TOP-10 mahalliy/mintaqaviy konferensiyalarning to'liq profilini o'z ichiga oladi.

* **Rasmiy URL:** [https://neurips.cc](https://neurips.cc)
* **Qamrov va Indekslash:** **CORE A\*** (Dunyoda #1 AI konferensiyasi), Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar Top Publications #1 AI.
